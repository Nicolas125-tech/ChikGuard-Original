⚡ Bolt: Optimize Background Worker Sleep for Graceful Shutdown

* 💡 What: Replaced time.sleep() with self._stop_event.wait() in cv_runner background thread.
* 🎯 Why: To prevent blocking the thread and allow immediate interruption and graceful shutdown when stop signals are received.
* 📊 Impact: Improves teardown time and responsiveness of the application during shutdown and test execution.
* 🔬 Measurement: Run application tests and observe faster shutdown times and lack of thread hanging warnings.

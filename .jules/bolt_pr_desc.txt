* 💡 What: Replaced blocking `time.sleep` with `threading.Event().wait` in the ContinuousAudioMonitor worker.
* 🎯 Why: Using `time.sleep()` blocks the thread and prevents immediate responses to stop signals, delaying graceful shutdowns. `Event.wait()` allows the thread to be instantly interrupted.
* 📊 Impact: Improves application responsiveness and shutdown times.
* 🔬 Measurement: Observe faster and cleaner shutdowns when the background worker is stopped.

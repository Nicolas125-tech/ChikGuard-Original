## 2024-11-20 - Accessible Stateful Toggle Buttons
**Learning:** In the DigitalTwinPanel view selectors, toggle buttons for different data layers (Mapa Térmico, Densidade, etc.) lacked ARIA attributes, making their active state invisible to screen readers. Furthermore, the inline lucide-react icons lacked `aria-hidden="true"`, causing redundant announcements since they were immediately followed by a descriptive `<span>`.
**Action:** When creating stateful view selectors or toggle buttons, use `aria-pressed={isActive}` on the `<button>` element to announce its state to screen readers. Always add `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative icons inside these buttons to reduce cognitive load and avoid redundant announcements.
